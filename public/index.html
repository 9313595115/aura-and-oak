Our Workshop

Where machine precision meets handcraft.

At Eagle CNC Art & Craft, every piece starts as a drawing on our screen and ends in a craftsman’s hands. Our CNC machines cut intricate patterns with accuracy no hand tool can match; then each piece is sanded, painted or polished by hand.

From a single name plate to a full set of temple panels, we work in MDF, plywood, solid wood and acrylic — and we’re always happy to make something that’s entirely yours.
