Questions

Good to know

Can’t find your answer? Message us — a real person replies.

How long will my order take?

Ready designs are dispatched in 2–3 working days. Personalised and made-to-order pieces take 3–10 working days to make, depending on the design — each product page shows an estimated delivery date for your PIN code.

Can you make a design in my size or with my own artwork?

Yes. Send a sketch, photo, logo or DXF/SVG file with the size you need. We reply with a design preview and price, and nothing is cut until you approve it.

Which materials do you work with?

Solid wood such as teak, MDF and HDHMR, birch and marine plywood, and clear, coloured or mirror acrylic. We’ll suggest the right one for indoor or outdoor use.

Do you deliver across India?

Yes — orders are packed in protective cartons and shipped with tracking. Large panels may travel with a transport partner; we confirm the timeline with your design.

Can I return a personalised piece?

Personalised and custom-sized pieces can’t be returned unless they arrive damaged or differ from the approved design. Ready designs can be returned within the return window — see our returns policy.

How do I look after wooden pieces?

Dust with a soft dry cloth, keep indoor pieces away from direct water, and wipe outdoor name plates occasionally. Avoid harsh cleaners on painted or stained finishes.
