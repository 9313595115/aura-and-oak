Shipping Information

Everything you need to know about how your order gets to you.

Processing Time

Orders are processed and packed within 1–2 business days. You'll receive a confirmation email as soon as your order is placed, and a shipping notification once it's on its way.

Order Tracking

Once your order ships, you'll receive an email with tracking details. You can also view order status any time from your Account page.

Large & Furniture Items

Furniture pieces like the Reclaimed Oak Side Table ship via a specialty freight carrier and may take 2–4 weeks depending on your location. We'll be in touch to schedule delivery.

International Shipping

We currently ship within the country selected at checkout. If you're outside our standard shipping zones and would like to place an order, reach out and we'll do our best to help.

Questions?

Read our returns policy, look up an order, or contact us — we're happy to help track down a delivery.
