Returns & Exchanges

We want you to love what you bring home. Here's how returns work.

30-Day Return Window

If something isn't right, you can return most items within 30 days of delivery for a full refund to your original payment method, provided the item is unused and in its original packaging.

How to Start a Return

  • Find your order in your Account page, or locate your order confirmation email.
  • Message us with your order number and which item(s) you'd like to return — or bring it to the store.
  • We'll send a prepaid return label and instructions.
  • Once we receive and inspect the item, your refund is issued within 5–7 business days.

Exceptions

A few categories can't be returned for hygiene or made-to-order reasons: candles that have been burned, and any custom or personalized pieces. Furniture returns may be subject to a return shipping fee due to freight costs.

Damaged or Incorrect Items

If your order arrives damaged or isn't what you ordered, contact us within 7 days with a photo and we'll send a replacement or refund at no cost to you — no return necessary in most cases.

Exchanges

Prefer a different size, color, or piece entirely? Let us know when you start your return and we'll get the replacement moving as soon as we receive the original.

Frequently Asked

Do I have to pay for return shipping?

No — standard items ship back on a prepaid label we provide. Freight furniture returns may include a shipping fee, which we'll always disclose up front.

When will I get my refund?

Refunds are issued within 5–7 business days of us receiving your return, back to your original payment method.

Can I return a gift?

Yes — reach out with the order number (found on the packing slip) and we'll help you sort out a return or exchange.
